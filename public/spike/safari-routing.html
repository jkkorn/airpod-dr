Spike v2: can the browser measure AirPods on iOS?

Early data suggests current iOS Safari lifted the limits the design assumed. This version decides each question with a measured verdict instead of asking your ear. Run it in Safari on your iPhone. Do the tests in order and read the colored verdicts.

Test 1 · no microphone needed

Output device control

Does this browser let a page choose which device audio plays out of? (This one does not touch the mic.)

Not run yet.
Test 2 · AirPods IN your ears

Does opening the mic kick sound off the AirPods?

Put both AirPods in. Tap Start. It plays a loud tone and measures how loud the phone mic hears it for 4 seconds. Quiet = sound stayed sealed in your AirPods (good). Loud = it jumped to the phone speaker.

Not run yet.
Test 3 · ONE AirPod OUT, ON THE PHONE MIC

Can the phone mic actually record the bud?

Take ONE AirPod out of your ear. Press its speaker directly onto the bottom edge of the phone, right by the charging port where the mic is. Not near your ear — the two have to touch so the phone mic can hear the bud. Tap. It plays a 20 Hz→16 kHz sweep to that bud and records it, then reports the capture rate and the low / mid / high energy captured. Highs present = the driver and the recording chain both work full-band.

Not run yet.
