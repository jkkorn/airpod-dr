Spike: does iOS Safari let us measure AirPods?

Approach B (the measured per-bud frequency-response fingerprint) needs to play a sweep to the AirPods while recording. This page tests whether a browser can do that on iOS, or whether the microphone forces audio off the AirPods. Run it in Safari on your iPhone, with AirPods connected. Read the log at the bottom.

1. setSinkId (output device selection)

If unsupported, a web page cannot even choose to keep output on the AirPods.

2. Does opening the mic move the sound?

Tap Play tone. You should hear a steady tone in your AirPods. Then tap Open microphone and listen: does the tone jump to the phone speaker? Record what you heard.


3. Loopback capture (sample rate is the tell)

Plays a sweep for 3s while recording from the microphone, then reports the captured sample rate and level. 16 kHz means iOS collapsed to the telephony (HFP) path; 44.1/48 kHz means full-band capture survived.
